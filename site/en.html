For Windows 10 & 11 · speech recognition and voices run locally · MIT license

What Julia can do

A program that keeps running on your computer and waits for instructions – via chat, hotkey or “Hey Julia”.

🖥️

Sees & acts

Screenshots of all monitors, clicks, types, opens programs, writes and moves files, runs PowerShell.

🎙️

Listens & speaks

Whisper understands you, a natural voice (Piper) replies – both offline on your PC.

🚦

Asks first

Every action goes through the traffic light: green just happens, yellow needs your yes, red never.

🧩

Small helpers

Media & volume, snap windows, timer & stopwatch, calculate, convert, QR codes.

⧉

Extensible via MCP

Julia uses the tools of any MCP server – every call asks first.

📱

On your phone too

Standalone Android and iOS app; optionally connects to your PC on your home network/VPN.

Plays Minecraft with you

As a character of its own on your server: follows, protects, mines, hunts, crafts, builds – and on request plays its own way toward the Ender Dragon.

🧠

Thinks along

Spots lava and drops, eats on its own, fights smart – retreats when low, keeps distance from creepers.

🎯

Plays through

With !spiel durch she works her own way along a tech tree up to the Ender Dragon.

📔

Daily logbook

Every step is written to disk immediately – survives crashes, so a play day is never lost.

Works with your apps

Connect Julia to your own apps – enter the domain and your login, done.

✓

ToDoch

“Put milk on my ToDoch list” – Julia creates the task.

▶

Streamo

“Add Iron Man to my Streamo list” – find a title and add it.

◆

VibeWork

Create projects, invite people and fetch the latest commit.

The traffic light

Julia rates every action itself – you stay in control.

Green

Read, look, orb – just happens.

Yellow

Write, install, send – only with your yes.

Red

Destructive things & payment data – never.

Get Julia

No admin rights, just for your user account. Keys are stored encrypted with Windows.
